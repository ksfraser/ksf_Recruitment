# Business Requirements - ksf_Recruitment

## Project Overview
ksf_Recruitment provides applicant tracking, candidate management, and hiring workflow.

| ksf_Workflow | Hiring approvals |

### Consumed From

|--------|---------------|

| ksf_Workflow | Approval workflows |
